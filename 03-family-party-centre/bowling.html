Cosmic bowling

Every Friday and Saturday from 7pm the lanes glow, the lights go down and the music goes up. $24 a person for two hours, shoes included.

Cosmic bowling uses black lights and coloured lighting. If anyone in your group is sensitive to changing light, call us and we'll tell you exactly what to expect.

Good to know

Do you have bumpers?

Yes, on every lane, and they can be set for individual bowlers on a shared lane.

What's the lightest ball?

Our lightest house ball is 6 lb, and we have ball ramps at the desk.

Is the centre step-free?

Yes: the entrance, all 24 lanes, the arcade, party rooms and restrooms are on one level.

How are rental shoes cleaned?

Every pair is sprayed, wiped and aired before it goes back on the shelf.
