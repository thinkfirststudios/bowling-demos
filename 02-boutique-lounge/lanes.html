Good to know

Before you book

What's the age policy?

All ages until 7pm with an adult. From 7pm it's 21+ with photo ID.

Can I just walk in?

Lanes 9 and 10 are kept for walk-ins on weeknights. Weekends are usually fully booked, so reserve ahead.

Are shoes included?

Yes, shoe rental is included with every lane booking.

Is there step-free access?

The entrance, bar, restrooms and lanes 1–6 are step-free. We keep a ball ramp and lightweight balls at the desk.

Do the lights change at night?

From 9pm Friday and Saturday the lanes switch to coloured lighting. There's no strobe; call us if you'd like to know more before booking.
