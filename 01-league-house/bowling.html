Good to know

Before you come

Do I need to book?

No. Open bowling is first come, first served at the front desk. Groups of 12 or more can reserve lanes on the Parties page.

Can I bring my own ball and shoes?

Yes. Bowling shoes only on the approach, please. Street shoes damage the surface.

Do you have bumpers and ball ramps?

Yes. Bumpers can be set on any lane, and we keep two ball ramps and 6 lb balls at the desk. Ask when you check in.

Is the building step-free?

The entrance, lanes 1–16, the grill and the restrooms are step-free. Lanes 17–32 are two steps down; ask us and we'll put you on a step-free lane.

How are rental shoes cleaned?

Every returned pair is sprayed, wiped down and left to air before it goes back on the shelf.
