Welcome to Hallstead

Fifty years of strikes, spares and Tuesday nights

Hallstead Lanes opened on Commerce Avenue in 1974 with sixteen lanes and a lunch counter. Today it's 32 lanes, a full bar and grill, a pro shop and seven leagues that fill the house Monday to Thursday.

Walk-ins are always welcome. On league nights we keep lanes 25–32 open for open bowling until 9:30pm, and the whole house opens up after.
